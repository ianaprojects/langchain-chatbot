# Skyline Belgrade Parking

Skyline Belgrade Parking is an indoor parking facility located in central Belgrade, within the Skyline tower complex.

---

## Location

Address: Kneza Miloša 90, Belgrade, Serbia.

The parking garage is part of the Skyline building.  
The entrance is located on the right side of the main tower.

---

## Nearby Landmarks

The parking is located near several recognizable places:

- Coffee Time café (across the street from the entrance)
- Skyline Business Center (same building complex)
- Kneza Miloša business district

Coffee Time café can be used as a visual reference point when searching for the entrance.

---

## Entrance and Directions

When driving along Kneza Miloša street:

- Look for the Skyline tower building
- The parking entrance ramp is on the right-hand side of the building
- The entrance is located near Coffee Time café, across the street

The entrance is clearly marked with parking signs and a downward ramp.

## Pricing

Parking pricing:

- 200 RSD per hour
- Maximum daily rate: 2500 RSD
- Monthly subscription: 15,000 RSD

Monthly subscriptions are available for regular users and must be arranged in person at the parking desk.

Keywords: price, cost, pricing, hourly rate, daily rate.

---

## Payment Methods and Rules

Payment is made at the exit terminal before leaving the parking facility.

Accepted payment methods include:
- cash
- credit cards
- debit cards

Drivers must pay before exiting the parking.

Online or advance payment is NOT available.

Payment rules:
- payment is required before exit
- the barrier opens after payment
- receipts are issued after payment

Keywords: payment, pay, payment methods, accepted payment methods, how to pay, cash, card, credit card, debit card.
